Hold a key, talk, release — your words appear where you were typing. blurt runs the NVIDIA Parakeet speech model entirely on-device on Apple Silicon via MLX, transcribing in 60–360ms after key release. There is no cloud, no account, and no audio leaving the machine — which is exactly what makes it usable in environments where cloud dictation is a non-starter. Delivery via the clipboard means it works in any app, including VDI and remote-desktop sessions where accessibility APIs don't reach.
